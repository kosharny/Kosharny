## About Me

I am a Mobile Developer building native applications for iOS and Android.

My commercial experience is focused on iOS development in a product company and freelance client projects, including scoped Android development tasks. My work covers interface implementation, API and SDK integrations, local persistence, authentication, in-app purchases, debugging, and App Store delivery.

Alongside client and product work, I develop personal applications with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, as well as SwiftUI applications and SpriteKit games. These projects let me explore reactive state management, database design, background work, device sensors, game logic, and platform services.

I enjoy turning an application idea into a working product: organizing its structure, implementing user flows, handling loading and error states, preserving user data, and preparing builds for delivery.

- **App maintenance:** investigate interface, data, and build issues; fix bugs; refine existing code; and verify changes in simulators and on physical devices.

## Featured Android Projects

### DriveCare
**Vehicle expenses, fuel tracking, and maintenance history**

A native Android application that brings vehicle records, ownership costs, and upcoming maintenance into one place. Core tracking works locally, with optional online AI guidance and a lifetime Pro purchase integration.

- Vehicle profiles, fuel records, service history, expenses, and attachments.
- Maintenance plans with date and mileage thresholds, recurring intervals, and reminders.
- Spending statistics, CSV export, ZIP backup/restore, and Pro PDF reports.
- Optional Firebase AI Logic guidance and RevenueCat purchase flows.

**Stack:** Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Material 3 · MVVM · Room · DataStore · Hilt · Coroutines · StateFlow · Navigation 3 · WorkManager · Firebase AI Logic · RevenueCat · Coil

[View repository](https://github.com/kosharny/DriveCare) · [▶ Watch the demo on YouTube](https://youtu.be/FaYQmUfEQPc)
